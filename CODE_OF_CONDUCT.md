# Code of conduct

KRYPTARA is a research project where findings against the design are welcome. The rules below keep
that criticism useful.

## Expected
- Criticise claims, proofs, code and designs, not people.
- Back a disagreement with a check, a counterexample or a source.
- Say plainly when something has not been run, measured or reviewed.
- Credit others' findings by name in the finding's record.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or doxxing.
- Presenting unrun work as done, or inventing results or sources.
- Publishing credentials, or attack procedures against hardware protections (see SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement
The maintainer, Kiruthik M R, may edit, hide or remove contributions and block participants who
break these rules. Report a concern through a GitHub issue, or by contacting the maintainer through
the profile linked from the repository.
