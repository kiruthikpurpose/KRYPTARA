# ADR-005: Presence as an evidence atom

**Status:** Accepted with open risk  
**Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Legacy IDs:** Legacy: D-25, J-33

## Context
Only non-cryptographic class available everywhere; restores safety and liveness under family loss.

## Decision
Sampled by locked stage; required for T2 in terrestrial profiles; never sufficient alone.

## Consequences
Correlated presence (F-R2-04) open; hardware verification pending.

## Alternatives considered
Process-only presence; presence-only recovery
