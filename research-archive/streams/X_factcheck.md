**Evidence tags:**
- **[P]**: a primary page or document, fetched.
- **[S]**: a secondary page, fetched.
- **[L]**: seen only in a search listing (the title and URL came from the primary site), not fetched.

## Notes

1. **Nothing in the blueprint is outright wrong. These caveats change how claims should be cited:**
   - **IR 8547 (#6).** The final deprecates *all* strengths of the quantum-vulnerable algorithms after 2030, not just 112-bit. This is from a secondary source.
   - **Fitzi–Hirt–Maurer CRYPTO '98 (#10).** The mixed-adversary bound is wrong as stated. See Damgård, BRICS RS-99-2.
   - **Trillian map (#14).** It was experimental, the current README no longer describes it, and Trillian itself is in maintenance mode.
   - **Kelsey et al. (#24).** Cite the full title.
2. **Things to re-read in the full documents.** These were not checked from the primary text:
   - FIPS 205 §11 instantiations (#3).
   - The NSA CNSA 2.0 PDFs and FAQ (#5). This includes B_crypto.md's claims of "single-tree only", that HashML-DSA is prohibited, and its transition dates.
   - The final IR 8547 PDF's deprecation table (#6).
   - Any FIPS 206 or FIPS 207 draft released after Apr or Aug 2026 (#1).
3. **Cross-check with the stream files:**
   - B_crypto.md line 14 says NIST "said in Aug 2025" that the FIPS 206 draft was submitted. A newer NIST statement exists, dated 13 Apr 2026 ("administrative approval chain").
   - B_crypto lines 17–18 (SP 800-230 and IR 8547) match these findings.
   - F_fleet_protocols and X_priorart on RFC 9943 (June 2026) match.
   - L_landscape on TPM v1.85 matches: TCG lists Version 185, March 2026.
4. **Limits of the evidence:**
   - Rows tagged [L] depend on search-listing titles and URLs from the primary or index site. They confirm titles, venues and dates, but not page ranges.
   - Figures read with WebFetch went through a summarising model.
   - Some pages failed: Springer article pages, media.defense.gov, dblp (bot protection) and the CSRC FIPS 206 page.
